## 2. Derivation History & Quarantine Rationale

1. **Derivation Method**: During Week 5 Phase B, these arrays were computed by applying Euclidean L2 normalization ($v / \max(\|v\|_2, 10^{-12})$) to pre-existing historical unnormalized validation arrays:
   - `trained_val_sar_embeddings.npy` (SHA256: `419dd3ced4bbcfef1cc98c0402fff2406dbf8b3f583eeec9a2e45d4b61c1535d`)
   - `trained_val_ms_embeddings.npy` (SHA256: `d053bb123dd96dddc1aa12e69d049ee4fb54b3fb002e0cb5efcbdf1a2d23fe2e`)
2. **Unverified Checkpoint Provenance**: The historical source arrays were inherited from early student contributor experiments, but their exact checkpoint weight provenance cannot be verified in the local environment because the physical `best_model-v1.ckpt` binary is unavailable.
3. **Not Inference Outputs**: These arrays were not generated by running forward passes through `TwoTowerNetwork` during Phase B. They must **never** be cited as evidence of successful checkpoint loading or neural network execution.
4. **Quarantine Rationale**: Relocated from `results/local_pc/week_05/trained_embeddings/` into this dedicated quarantine directory during Phase B.2 to eliminate any potential naming confusion or risk of accidental consumption in downstream evaluation scripts.
5. **Preservation**: Retained strictly for mathematical audit trail and reproducibility.
